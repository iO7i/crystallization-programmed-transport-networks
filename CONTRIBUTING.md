# Contributing

Useful contributions should address literature reproduction, numerical validation,
morphology generators or adapters, transport methods, topology analysis, scientific
documentation, or reproducibility infrastructure.

For a scientific contribution, state:

1. What claim is being tested?
2. What changed?
3. How was it validated?
4. What assumptions were introduced?

Please do not add a new model, metric, or dependency without a validation fixture and
an explicit statement of its physical scope.
