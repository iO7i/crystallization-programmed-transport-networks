# Open questions

1. Which descriptors remain predictive when the same transport phase fraction is
   produced by different crystallization histories rather than thresholded fixtures?
2. When does crystallization stabilize a conducting network, and when does it reduce
   mobility or sever connectivity?
3. How should interfacial resistance and phase-specific mobility be calibrated for a
   specified transport mechanism?
4. Which SCFT or field-theoretic morphology outputs can be coupled to a crystallization
   model without treating equilibrium SCFT as crystallization kinetics?
5. How much of the transport tensor is controlled by topology versus domain width,
   interface area, and orientation under experimentally meaningful controls?
6. Can any architecture/process-to-morphology surrogate generalize outside the material
   family used for training and calibration?
7. What experimental data and uncertainty model would be sufficient to test a forward
   architecture plus processing plus crystallization-history model?
