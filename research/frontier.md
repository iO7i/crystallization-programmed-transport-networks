# Research frontier

## What the focused audit supports

The literature supports each link as a family of questions, but not as one validated
universal chain. Crystalline blocks in block copolymers can crystallize in confined,
templated, or breakout modes, and crystallization can preserve, distort, or reorganize
the pre-existing microdomain morphology. Related crystalline-polymer blend work also
shows that growth order, interpenetrating spherulites, crystallinity, and segmental
mobility are coupled in system-specific ways.

Transport work demonstrates that connectivity, topology, and path geometry affect
diffusion-like behavior. Some studies link SCFT-generated morphologies to random-walk
transport, but that is still a selected model family, not a general crystallization
history model.

## Boundary of the current claim

This repository therefore makes one narrower, reproducible contribution first:
measure morphology-to-transport effects in explicitly defined synthetic voxel
geometries. Benchmark 001 does not claim to simulate a polymer, crystallization,
segmental mobility, ionic conductivity, membrane permeability, or any PES/PEO-related
material.

The most important unresolved question is whether a small set of morphology descriptors
can retain predictive value when the morphology is generated by a physically calibrated
crystallization model rather than by an analytical or synthetic fixture.
